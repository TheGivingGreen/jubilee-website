OUR STORY · SINCE 1991

It started with a simple thought: we could do this better.

When Bill and Judy McNeill's kids played sports, the team uniforms left a lot to be desired. It became a running family joke. In 1991, they stopped joking and started printing. As Judy puts it, “The Lord looks after fools.”

Bill left his job. Judy learned to screen print. They built JuBilee one order and one customer at a time.

Today, Judy and her daughter Kim carry that work forward right here in West End. They make custom apparel and personalized products for the businesses, schools, teams, and neighbors who make the Sandhills home.

The equipment has changed. The possibilities have grown. But their favorite part? Watching someone love their new shirt so much they change into it before walking out the door.

A GOOD PLACE TO START

A few common questions.

Do I need to know which printing method I want?+

No. Tell us what you want to make and share your artwork. We’ll help you choose a method suited to the design and product.

Can you help us choose the apparel?+

Yes. JuBilee works with apparel distributors and can help you find the right product for your project.

How do group stores work?+

Your group orders and pays online. JuBilee handles ordering, printing, sorting, and individually bagging the orders. Contact us to discuss your store and pickup arrangements.
